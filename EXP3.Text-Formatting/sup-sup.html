Superscript and Subscript

Superscript and subscript tags are used to display text slightly above or below the normal line of text. They are commonly used in mathematical expressions and chemical formulas.

Subscript

Subscript text appears slightly below the normal line.
Example: H2O (Water), CO2 (Carbon Dioxide)

Superscript

Superscript text appears slightly above the normal line.
Example: X2, a2 + b2

These tags help in correctly representing scientific and mathematical data in HTML.
